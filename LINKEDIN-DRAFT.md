# LinkedIn draft — software release, human study pending

I built ED Roster Gap Lab to make one question testable: when does a formal staffing-rule checker agree with a clinician's judgment about a roster?

The public workbench checks synthetic rosters, reports each finding against an explicit rule, and prepares a blinded labeling workflow. It also tests whether its own checks can catch deliberately planted defects.

The distinction matters: the current demonstration uses hypothetical rules and simulated labels. It proves that the pipeline runs; it does not establish clinical agreement or safer staffing. The human-label study still needs my reviewed rules and judgments before its original endpoint is complete.

The repository includes runnable examples, methods, limitations, a correction record, and a software/protocol manuscript. No staff or patient records are used.
